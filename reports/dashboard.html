Could this test have seen a real signal?

With 988 test days the AUC has a standard error of about 0.018 when there is no signal. A signal must give AUC of about 0.530 to be noticed, and about 0.546 to be noticed 80% of the time.

We planted signals of known strength in the real sentiment series and ran the same code. With nothing planted it raised a false alarm 5% of the time, as it should. A planted signal worth AUC 0.55 was found 89% of the time.

So effects much larger than this are ruled out; very small ones are not.
